      Luhn Algorithm 
 ***********************
 step1: divide the it in groups of 4 digits
 step2: double every first digit from left to right 
        if the doubled number is 2 digits split them.
 step3: add all single digit numbers to create a sum
 step4: add all even positioned numbers from left to right from the original number
 step5: sum the results from step 2 and 3
 step6: if step 5 is divisible by 10 that # is valid
